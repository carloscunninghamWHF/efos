Methodological safeguards:
- Data -> Metrics -> Interpretation -> Evidence -> Narrative
- EVENT_PROXY is explicitly separated from 360/tracking.
- No real TTI is asserted without tracking.
- Trap Engine is labeled EXPERIMENTAL.
- DefR is treated as defensive responsibility proxy, not physical pressure intensity.
- Coach patterns are observed behavior, not proof of coach intent.
- Without match dates, temporal blocks use dataset order and are labeled accordingly.
